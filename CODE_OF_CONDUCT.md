# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue with the work, not the person. Bring evidence.

## Scope

This applies to everything in this repository and its issues and pull
requests.

## Expected behaviour

- **Disagree about the change, not the person.** "That is wrong" is a fine
  thing to say here; say what you saw and how.
- **Assume the other person read the docs and still disagrees.** They may have
  found something.
- **Accept correction the same way you give it.**

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information - including in-game family or
  character names - without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## A rule specific to this project

**Do not ask for, or contribute, anything that touches the game process.** No
memory reading, no injection, no input automation, no macros, no packet or
client-file access. That boundary is permanent and is not open to negotiation
in an issue; requests to relax it, or contributions that quietly cross it, will
be closed. The stake is somebody's account.

## Reporting

Raise a concern by opening an issue, or - if it involves a specific person and
should not be public - through GitHub's own
[reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

The maintainer will review what is reported and decide what to do, which may
include editing or deleting comments, closing a thread or blocking an account.
This is a one-person project; there is no committee and no appeals process.
